Selection + Highlights

Try selecting this text — the selection uses light blue rgba(99,102,241,0.3). Highlights render in their own vivid colors and are not affected by the selection color.

In the app, mix-blend-mode: multiply is removed from highlight SVG fills so the selection blue doesn't bleed through. Highlights use solid fill-opacity: 0.55 instead.

In Practice

She walked through the quiet forest, the morning light filtering through the canopy of ancient oaks. A sense of profound stillness settled over her as she followed the winding path deeper into the woods.

The old cottage stood at the edge of a clearing, its windows glowing with warm amber light. She had memorized every detail from her grandmother's stories — the crooked chimney, the weathered door, the roses climbing the trellis.

Highlights use a soft highlighter-style fill in four vivid colors. Underlines are a thin red line (2px) positioned just below the text baseline — clean and minimal.
